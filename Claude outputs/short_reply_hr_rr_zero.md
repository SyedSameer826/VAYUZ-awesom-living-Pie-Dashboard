The heart rate and respiration rate showing 0 at 04:30 AM and 08:00 AM on the chart is not a medical concern — it simply means the person was off the bed pad during those time windows (likely bathroom visits). When the person steps off the sensor, both readings naturally go to zero since there is no body to measure. The values return to normal in the very next interval once they are back in bed.

This is expected behavior for a bed-based sensor. No action needed.
